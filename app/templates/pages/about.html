About Student Saarthi

Dedicated to helping students across India discover, check eligibility, and apply for government and private scholarships without barriers.

Our Mission

To eliminate financial obstacles to education by connecting students directly with verified central, state, and private scholarship programs through a simple, transparent portal.

Our Vision

An India where no deserving student has to discontinue their education due to lack of financial guidance, complex paperwork, or missed scholarship application dates.

Our Promise

100% verified opportunities, authentic government links, and a free platform for students with complete data privacy.
