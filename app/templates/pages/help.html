Help Centre & FAQ

Answers to common questions about registration, eligibility criteria, applications, and verification.

How do I register on Student Saarthi?

Click on "Register" in the navigation bar. Complete your personal identity details, contact information, education level, current percentage, and family income band. Once completed, your profile is immediately initialized with tailored recommendations.

How does the eligibility checker work?

Our matching system compares your profile credentials (academic scores, education level, state domicile, category, and income band) directly against official rules of each scheme to show whether you qualify before applying.

How do I apply for a scholarship?

Navigate to any scholarship in the directory and click "Apply". Review your pre-filled details, write your Statement of Purpose (minimum 50 characters), attach supporting documents (e.g. marksheets, income certificate), accept the declaration, and submit.

How can I track my submitted application?

Open "My Applications" in your student menu. Real-time status indicators display whether your application is Submitted, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected alongside direct remarks from administrators.

Are featured scholarships authentic?

Yes. Every scheme listed in our repository is cross-verified against official Central Government portals, state higher education departments, and accredited corporate CSR foundations.
