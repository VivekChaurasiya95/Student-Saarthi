Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Strict confidentiality and student data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect

Student Saarthi collects information required to evaluate scholarship eligibility and manage applications, including:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, and address.
  • Academic records: Current education level, examination percentages, institution name, and field of study.
  • Background: Reservation category and annual family income.
  • Application materials: Statements of purpose, achievement summaries, and uploaded verification documents.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Match your student profile with qualifying scholarship opportunities.
  • Process and submit your applications for verification.
  • Provide notifications on deadlines and application status updates.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent duplicate accounts.

3. Data Security

All credentials and passwords are encrypted using secure industry-standard hashing algorithms before storage. Application documents are stored securely with restricted access control. We never sell, rent, or trade student personal information to commercial advertising or third-party marketing networks.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at privacy@studentsaarthi.com.
